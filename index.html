Rishab Gautam — Software Developer & Entrepreneur · Co-Founder & DevOps Engineer at Nept Cloud

Rishab Gautam (also known as rishabnotfound) is a software developer and entrepreneur based in Delhi, India, born on 22 January 2007. He is the Co-Founder & DevOps Engineer at Nept Cloud, a developer-focused cloud platform for deploying and managing modern applications, and ships production-grade infrastructure used by hundreds of thousands of people worldwide. He also contributes upstream to major open-source projects including Node.js, PreMiD, and FMHY.

About Rishab

Rishab works end-to-end across product, infrastructure, and low-level systems. His public work includes Layla, a free web-push platform powering 50–60 major sites with a combined ~160,000 subscribers and 256,800+ notifications delivered, engineered to handle millions of requests per second; Syella, a cross-platform Windows & macOS terminal designed to out-perform MobaXterm and Termius combined; MakimaKey, a fully-offline, end-to-end-encrypted TOTP authenticator for Android running on 3–4k devices across every supported Android version; DeltaSys, a browser-based real-time VPS control plane with full SSH and xterm.js terminal emulation; Commitify, a production-grade GitHub contribution-graph automation tool used by hundreds of developers; MongoSync, a modern MongoDB administration surface; RezePlayer, a standalone HLS/MP4 video engine with watch-party sync, Chromecast, and automatic thumbnail generation; and Maki-HLS-Proxy, the world's first HLS proxy written in pure Lua + nginx. Underneath these products he focuses on reverse-engineering closed protocols, applied cryptography, resilient scraping pipelines, and HLS streaming infrastructure. He is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering at IILM University, Gurugram.
